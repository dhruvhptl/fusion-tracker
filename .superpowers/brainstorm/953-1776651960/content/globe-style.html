Globe visual treatment

The PRD says "observatory interface, not flashy sci-fi." Within that direction, which Earth texture feels right?

A

Dark topographic — muted landmasses

Near-black oceans, dim slate-blue continents outlined but not detailed. Feels scientific and restrained. Markers pop.

B

NASA night-lights Earth

Real satellite imagery of city lights on a dark Earth. Atmospheric, ties markers to where humans actually are. Slightly more visual noise.

C

Wireframe / hex-grid

Pure geometric globe — no satellite imagery, just a glowing lat/lng grid or hex tessellation. Most abstract, most "observatory UI."
