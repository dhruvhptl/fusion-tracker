When the sidebar opens, what does the globe do?

The drawer slides in from the right. The clicked marker's company has 1-3 highlighted locations. We need to decide how much the globe reacts.

A

Globe stays put, auto-rotate pauses

No camera movement. Only change: all markers for that company warm up to the "selected" color. User can still drag to explore. Minimal, respects user's current view.

B

Globe gently rotates to center the clicked marker

Smooth ~800ms rotation so the clicked dot sits left-of-center (drawer takes the right third). Other company markers glow wherever they are. Feels responsive.

C

Globe zooms + centers on the company's markers collectively

Camera frames all of the company's dots (e.g., CFS's HQ + Virginia plant). Most cinematic, but can feel jerky for globally spread companies.
