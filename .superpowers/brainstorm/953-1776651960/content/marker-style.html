How should HQs and facilities look different?

15 companies with ~8 extra facility markers means ~23 dots on the globe. The user needs to tell HQ apart from facility at a glance without hovering.

A

Size — HQ bigger, facility smaller

Same color (teal/cyan), HQ dot ~1.5× larger. Simple, readable, reinforces the "company has one HQ and several sites" hierarchy.

B

Shape — HQ solid dot, facility hollow ring

Same size, same color. Visually distinct without a hierarchy. Works well when HQ and facility are colocated.

C

Altitude — HQ on surface, facility slightly raised

Both dots, but facility floats a bit above the globe surface (tiny 3D offset). Subtle but reads clearly on a rotating globe.

D

Don't differentiate — handle it in the sidebar

All markers identical on the globe. The sidebar tells you HQ vs. facility. Cleanest look, but loses info density.
